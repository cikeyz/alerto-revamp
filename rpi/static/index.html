How this dashboard works

Four ceiling sensors (Seg A–D) measure how much of the floor under them is occupied. The hub turns that into a crowd level for each segment and forecasts the next 30 minutes every 5 minutes.

The platform display tells commuters which zone has more room. When every segment is High, the entry gate closes until one segment drops below High.
